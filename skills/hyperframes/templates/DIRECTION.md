## Waivers (the escape hatch — a conscious choice on the record)

A gate you deliberately decline. Format (the gate parses this line exactly):

```
WAIVER: <gate-id> — <one-sentence reason>
```

Valid `gate-id`s: `assets` · `registers` · `spatial_canvas` · `fast_passages` · `sound_plan` · `direction` · `opening` · `three` · `audit` · `bgm`. Example: `WAIVER: assets — pure-typographic register is the argument; generated plates would dilute the "just the words" thesis.` Admissions, malformed manifests, unsafe paths, stale timing hashes, and broken media wiring are never waivable. Artistic omission and missing rendered-review evidence may be waived with a concrete reason.
